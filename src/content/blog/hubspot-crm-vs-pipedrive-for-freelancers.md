HubSpot CRM and Pipedrive are two of the best-known tools for keeping track of leads and clients. Both show you who you are talking to and what stage each deal is at. They take different approaches, and for a freelancer the biggest difference is price: HubSpot starts free, while Pipedrive does not.

The short version: pick HubSpot CRM if you want a capable tool for free and may grow into more features later. Pick Pipedrive if your work is mostly chasing and closing new leads, and you want a simple pipeline you can set up in a day or two. Free plans, limits and prices change often, so check each company's pricing page before you commit.

## At a glance

| | HubSpot CRM | Pipedrive |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | Yes, with limits | No, only a 14-day trial |
| Paid plans | Roughly $15 to $20 per seat a month for the first paid tier | Roughly $14 per user a month for Lite, billed annually |
| Focus | A broad suite covering sales, marketing and service | A sales pipeline |
| Setup | Quick for the basics | Quick, and built around the pipeline |
| Best for | Freelancers who want a free CRM that can grow | Freelancers who chase many leads |

## Pricing

HubSpot's CRM has a permanent free plan that covers contacts, a deal pipeline and email tracking. Sources disagree about its exact limits, with some mentioning contact caps and some mentioning user caps, so check what the free plan includes today. The first paid tier is reported at roughly $15 to $20 per seat per month. Be careful with the higher plans, which cost much more, and one source notes that some top tiers require a large one-time onboarding fee.

Pipedrive has no permanent free plan, only a 14-day trial. Its cheapest plan, Lite, is reported at around $14 per user a month when billed annually, and about $24 when billed monthly. Higher plans cost more, and add-ons for extra automation or reports are charged separately.

## How they work

Pipedrive is built around the pipeline. You see every lead as a card moving from "contacted" to "proposal sent" to "won," and the tool nudges you toward the next action. Reviews often describe it as simpler and quicker to learn than HubSpot.

HubSpot is a larger system. The free CRM covers the basics, but HubSpot also sells marketing, sales and customer service tools that connect to the same database. That is useful if you grow, but it also means more menus and more upsells as you use it.

## Which is better for freelancers?

- **If you want to pay nothing:** HubSpot, because it has a free plan and Pipedrive does not.
- **If you spend most of your time pitching new clients:** Pipedrive's focus on the pipeline can make follow-ups easier to see.
- **If you mostly work with a small group of repeat clients:** either one is probably more than you need. A simple tool or a spreadsheet may be enough, and our guide to the [best CRM tools for freelancers](/blog/best-crm-tools-for-freelancers/) covers lighter options.
- **If you plan to add marketing later:** HubSpot gives you more room to grow under one roof.

## Who should pick HubSpot CRM

- You want a free CRM to start with.
- You may want email tracking and more features later.
- You are comfortable with a tool that does more than you need today.

## Who should pick Pipedrive

- You chase a steady flow of new leads and want a clear visual pipeline.
- You are happy to pay a small monthly fee for a simpler tool.
- You prefer to focus on sales without the extra suite.

## Our recommendation

Start with HubSpot's free plan. It costs nothing, and you will learn what you actually need from a CRM before you spend money. If you find the free plan limits you, or you want a pipeline that is easier to follow, try Pipedrive's free trial and compare the two with your real leads.

Whichever you choose, use it every day for two weeks. The best CRM is the one you actually update.
