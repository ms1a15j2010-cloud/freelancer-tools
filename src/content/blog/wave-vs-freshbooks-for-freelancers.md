Wave and FreshBooks are two of the most popular tools for sending invoices and tracking income as a freelancer. They look similar on the surface, but they are priced very differently, and that difference decides who should pick which.

The short version: Wave is the better starting point if you want to pay nothing. FreshBooks is the better fit if you want more automation, more integrations and a smoother experience, and you are willing to pay for it. Prices and plans change often, so check each company's pricing page before you commit.

## At a glance

| | Wave | FreshBooks |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free starter plan, with an optional paid plan | Paid only, roughly $19 to $23 a month for the cheapest plan |
| Invoicing | Yes | Yes |
| Accounting | Basic, included | Light, included |
| Integrations | Limited | Many |
| Best for | Beginners on a tight budget | Freelancers who want more automation |

## Pricing

Wave's starter plan is free and covers invoicing and basic accounting. It earns money mainly from payment processing, so card payments from clients carry fees, and it also sells a paid plan with extra features. Sources report that plan at roughly $16 to $19 a month.

FreshBooks has no free plan, though it has offered a free trial. Sources list the cheapest plan anywhere from about $19 to $23 a month, and promotions change the price, so check the current figure. Cheaper plans limit how many clients you can bill, so check that limit against your client list.

## Features

Both tools let you send professional invoices, track expenses and see who still owes you money. The differences show up in what is built on top:

- **Automation:** FreshBooks offers recurring invoices, automatic payment reminders and late fee calculations. These save time if you bill the same clients regularly.
- **Integrations:** FreshBooks connects with many other tools, including project management apps. Wave has fewer direct integrations, and some connections go through a service such as Zapier.
- **Reporting:** Wave's reports cover the basics, such as income, expenses and tax records. FreshBooks goes further, though both are lighter than full accounting software.
- **Multiple currencies:** If you invoice international clients, check how each tool handles other currencies before you decide.

## Availability: check this first

Wave describes itself as serving small businesses in the United States and Canada, and its features can differ by country. FreshBooks is available more widely, but its features also vary by region. If you live elsewhere, confirm that your chosen tool works for you before you spend time setting it up.

## Who should pick Wave

- You are just starting out and want to pay nothing.
- You send a small number of invoices.
- You only need basic income and expense tracking.

## Who should pick FreshBooks

- You bill several clients regularly and want invoices and reminders to run themselves.
- You use other tools that FreshBooks connects with.
- You would rather pay for a smoother experience than do more by hand.

## Our recommendation

If you are not sure, start with Wave's free plan. You can move to FreshBooks when your client list grows and you want more automation. If you already know you want reminders, recurring invoices and integrations from day one, try FreshBooks' free trial and decide for yourself.
