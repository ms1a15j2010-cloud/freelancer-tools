Toggl Track and Clockify are the two time trackers most often recommended to freelancers. Both have a free plan, both let you start a timer in a click, and both turn your hours into reports. The differences are in polish, features and what you pay once you outgrow the free plan.

The short version: Toggl Track is the one to try first if you want the cleanest, most pleasant timer. Clockify is the one to try first if you want the most features for the least money. Free plans, limits and prices change often, so check each company's pricing page before you commit.

## At a glance

| | Toggl Track | Clockify |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | Yes, up to 5 users | Yes, up to 5 users |
| Paid plans | Roughly $9 to $18 per user a month | Roughly $4 to $10 per user a month |
| Known for | Clean design and a reliable timer | Generous features at a low price |
| Integrations | Many, including a browser extension | Many, though sources report fewer |
| Best for | Freelancers who value simplicity | Budget-conscious freelancers who want features |

## Pricing

Both tools have a free plan with unlimited time entries and projects. Several sources say the free plan is capped at 5 users for both, but some older pages say Clockify's is unlimited, so check the current limit. If you work alone, this should not matter.

The difference appears when you upgrade. Toggl Track's paid plans are reported at roughly $9 a month for the Starter plan and $18 for Premium, per user. Clockify's paid plans start lower, at roughly $4 to $5 a month, and its higher plans are reported at around $7 to $10. Prices are usually lower when billed annually, so compare like with like.

## Features

- **The timer:** Both let you start and stop a timer from the web, desktop and mobile. Toggl Track is often praised for a cleaner interface and a reliable automatic tracker that suggests entries based on what you worked on.
- **Reports:** Both give you reports by project and client. Clockify offers more reporting and cost tools as you move up its paid plans.
- **Billable rates and invoicing:** These are tied to Clockify's paid plans according to some sources, and sources differ on what is free, so check before you rely on it. If you need invoices, pair either tool with an invoicing tool. See our guide to the [best invoicing tools for freelancers](/blog/best-invoicing-tools-for-freelancers/).
- **Integrations:** Toggl Track is generally described as having more integrations, including a browser extension that works inside tools such as Jira, Notion and GitHub.
- **Extras:** Clockify has a kiosk feature for clocking in from a shared device, which matters for teams in physical workplaces, but not much for a solo freelancer.

## Who should pick Toggl Track

- You want the cleanest design and a pleasant daily experience.
- You like automatic suggestions for what you worked on.
- You are happy to pay a bit more if you upgrade later.

## Who should pick Clockify

- You want the most features at the lowest price.
- You expect to upgrade and want cheaper paid plans.
- You do not mind a busier interface.

## Our recommendation

For most freelancers working alone, the free plan on either tool is enough, so try both for a week and keep the one you actually remember to use. If you are undecided, start with Toggl Track for its simplicity. If cost is the main worry, or you expect to need more reporting later, start with Clockify.
