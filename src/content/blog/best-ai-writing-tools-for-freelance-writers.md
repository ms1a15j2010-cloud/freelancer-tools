AI writing tools can save a freelance writer hours on outlines, first drafts, rewrites and editing. They can also produce bland, wrong or repetitive text if you trust them blindly. The best way to use them is as an assistant that speeds up your process, while you stay responsible for the final work.

Here are seven popular options, what each is good for, and the trade-offs. Features, free plans and prices change often, so check each company's pricing page before you pay.

## Quick picks

- **Want one tool for research, outlines and drafts:** a general AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini
- **Want a final grammar and tone check:** Grammarly
- **Want marketing copy templates:** Jasper, Copy.ai or Rytr
- **Want to rework or paraphrase your own sentences:** QuillBot
- **Write SEO articles for clients:** Surfer SEO

## General AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)

General-purpose assistants are the most flexible option. They can brainstorm headlines, outline an article, summarize research, suggest edits and rewrite a paragraph in a different tone. Most have a free tier with limits and paid plans for heavier use. They are good at drafting but can state wrong facts with confidence, so verify every claim yourself.

**Good for:** most freelance writers, as a first tool to try.

## Grammarly

Grammarly checks spelling, grammar and tone as you write, and also offers AI suggestions. The free plan covers the basics, including a limited number of AI prompts each month, and paid plans cost roughly $12 a month. It does not write articles for you.

**Good for:** a final editing pass on any piece of writing.

## Jasper

Jasper is built for marketing teams and content creators, with templates, brand voice settings and campaign tools. It is more expensive than most options, with plans reported from roughly $40 to $60 a month, so make sure you need the marketing features.

**Good for:** freelancers writing a lot of marketing copy for brands.

## Copy.ai

Copy.ai focuses on marketing copy and sales content, with templates and workflow automation. Its free plan and limits have changed over time, so check what is included before you rely on it.

**Good for:** short-form copy such as ads, emails and product descriptions.

## Rytr

Rytr is a budget-friendly writing assistant with many templates and a free tier. Paid plans are reported to start at around $7.50 to $9 a month. It is simpler than the bigger tools, and its output usually needs more editing.

**Good for:** writers who want a cheap way to get first drafts of short pieces.

## QuillBot

QuillBot is known for paraphrasing and rewording text, and it has a free plan. It helps when you want to rework your own sentences or simplify a paragraph. It is not a tool for creating original articles from scratch.

**Good for:** polishing and rewording what you have already written.

## Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO helps with search-focused writing by showing which topics and terms top-ranking pages cover and scoring your draft against them. It is built for SEO content and costs more than general writing tools, so it only makes sense if clients ask for search-optimized articles.

**Good for:** freelancers who write blog content for clients that want to rank on Google.

## Use AI honestly

Keep these rules in mind:

1. **Check the facts.** AI tools can invent statistics, quotes and sources.
2. **Read your client's rules.** Some clients forbid AI-generated text or ask you to disclose it. Follow the contract.
3. **Edit heavily.** Unedited AI text is easy to spot and rarely sounds like you.
4. **Never paste confidential client material** into a tool unless you have checked its privacy terms.

## How to choose

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **What do I need help with: ideas, drafts, or editing?** Pick the tool for that step.
2. **Do my clients allow AI?** If not, stick to editing tools like Grammarly.
3. **Is a free plan enough?** For most writers starting out, a free assistant plus Grammarly's free plan is enough.

Try free versions first, and add a paid tool only when you hit a limit that costs you time.
