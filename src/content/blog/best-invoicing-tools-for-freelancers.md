Getting paid should not take longer than doing the work. A good invoicing tool sends professional invoices, reminds clients who forget to pay, and shows you who still owes you money. The right one depends on how many clients you have, whether you need accounting too, and how much you want to spend.

Here are six popular options, what each is good for, and the trade-offs. Prices change often, so check each company's pricing page before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Want to pay nothing:** Wave or Zoho Invoice
- **Want the most polished, hands-off experience:** FreshBooks
- **Want invoicing, contracts and projects in one place:** Bonsai
- **Technical and want control:** Invoice Ninja
- **Want the fastest way to send an invoice from your phone:** Invoice Simple

## Wave

Wave is the usual starting point for freelancers on a tight budget. Its free plan covers unlimited invoices and basic accounting, and a paid plan adds extra features. Card payments carry processing fees, as with most tools, so factor that in.

**Good for:** freelancers who want invoicing plus simple bookkeeping at no monthly cost.

## FreshBooks

FreshBooks is the tool most comparison sites rank as the best all-rounder. It handles invoices, automatic payment reminders and light accounting in a clean interface. Plans start at roughly $23 a month, and the cheapest plan limits how many clients you can bill, so check the limit against your client list.

**Good for:** freelancers who want billing handled with as little effort as possible and do not mind paying for it.

## Zoho Invoice

Zoho Invoice is free and includes recurring invoices, reminders, a client portal, and time and expense tracking. It supports several currencies, which helps if you work with international clients, and it connects well with other Zoho products.

**Good for:** freelancers who want a free tool with more features than the basics.

## Invoice Ninja

Invoice Ninja is open source. It has a free hosted plan for a small number of clients, and you can also host it yourself. It gives you more control than most tools, with a steeper learning curve.

**Good for:** developers and technical freelancers who like to control their own tools.

## Bonsai

Bonsai combines invoicing with contracts, proposals, time tracking and project management. Plans start at around $25 a month, and it has changed its pricing model recently, so read the current plan details carefully.

**Good for:** freelancers who want one tool for the whole client process, not only billing.

## Invoice Simple

Invoice Simple is built for speed. You can create and send an invoice from your phone in a few minutes. It starts at around $7 a month, with a free trial.

**Good for:** freelancers who send a few invoices and do not want accounting software.

## How to choose

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **Do I need accounting or only invoices?** If only invoices, pick something simple.
2. **How many clients do I bill each month?** Some plans cap the number of clients.
3. **Where do my clients live?** If they pay from other countries, check which currencies and payment methods the tool supports.

If you are just starting, begin with a free tool like Wave or Zoho Invoice. You can always move to a paid one when your client list grows.
